# Al Rostamani Salary Card Balance Check

If your salary lands on an Al Rostamani Exchange salary card (Rpay), checking the balance takes less than a minute once you know the right channel. This note covers the three methods workers in the UAE actually use: the online portal, the mobile route, and the ATM option.

## Online balance check

Head to the Rpay online portal, sign in with your card details, and your current balance appears on the dashboard along with recent salary credits and withdrawals. It is the best option when you want the full transaction history, not just one number.

## Mobile and SMS options

On the move, the mobile channel is quicker. Use the card issuer's app or SMS service tied to your registered mobile number to get the latest balance without visiting a branch or an exchange counter.

## ATM balance enquiry

Any supported ATM lets you check the balance on the spot. Insert the card, enter your PIN, and select balance enquiry. Take the receipt if you are about to make a purchase and want to be sure the funds are there.

## Salary credited but balance not updated?

Salary credits can take a short while to reflect after payday, especially around weekends and public holidays. Wait a few hours, then recheck online. If the credit still has not appeared after a full working day, contact Al Rostamani Exchange support with your card number and employer details ready.
