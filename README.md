# FAB Prepaid Cards Enquiry Guide

A short, practical reference for anyone in the UAE holding a First Abu Dhabi Bank prepaid card and wondering how to check what is left on it. This page walks through each official way to run a prepaid cards enquiry, what details you need on hand, and what to do when a balance looks wrong.

## Check your balance online

The fastest route for most cardholders is the bank's own online channel. Sign in to FAB online banking or open the FAB mobile app, go to the cards section, and pick the prepaid card you want to check. Your available balance and recent transactions show up right away, with no branch visit needed.

## Check by SMS and phone

If you are away from the internet, the phone options still work. FAB phone banking can confirm a card balance after identity verification, and SMS alerts keep you posted whenever money moves on the card. Keep your card number and registered mobile number ready before you call, since the agent will ask for both.

## At an ATM

Any FAB ATM in the UAE can print or display a prepaid card balance. Insert the card, enter the PIN, and choose the balance enquiry option. The receipt is handy if you need proof of the balance for a purchase or a refund discussion.

## What to do if the balance looks wrong

First, check pending transactions, since a recent purchase can take a day to settle. If the figure still does not add up, call FAB phone banking with the transaction dates and amounts ready, and ask them to trace the entries. Never share your full card number or PIN in a chat message or email while sorting this out.
